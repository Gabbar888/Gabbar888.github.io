Hi, I'm Subham — I just finished my B.S. in Statistics and Data Science (with a minor in CSE) at IIT Kanpur. My work sits across the AI stack, from the infrastructure side (training, post-training, and fine-tuning large language models) to building products that use them. I most recently interned at **Samsung Research Institute Bangalore**, where I built a multi-GPU fine-tuning pipeline for Qwen-3 14B/32B on AWS. Before that, I worked on Bayesian methods, neural machine translation, and a few smaller things — see [projects](/projects/) for details.

A view I hold: **AI should be the assistant, not the master, of your work — especially for developers, who shouldn't ship code they can't explain.** And: evaluations are the most undervalued thing in the current LLM ecosystem.

**Currently:** building an open-source pipeline for dense knowledge graph extraction from unstructured text, and thinking hard about how to benchmark it properly — because right now, no one really can do it really good and exhaustively.
