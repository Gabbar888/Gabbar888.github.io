**Incoming Developer at Samsung Research Institute Bangalore** 

Hi, I'm Subham — I just finished my B.S. in Statistics and Data Science (with a minor in CSE) at IIT Kanpur. My work sits across the AI stack, from the infrastructure side (training, post-training, and fine-tuning large language models) to building products that use them. I interned there last summer, building a multi-GPU fine-tuning pipeline for Qwen-3 14B/32B on AWS. Before that, I worked on Bayesian methods, neural machine translation, and a few smaller things — see [projects](/projects/) for details.

One thing I genuinely believe :**AI should be the assistant, not the master, of your work — especially for developers, who shouldn't ship code they can't explain.** And: evaluations are the most undervalued thing in the current LLM ecosystem.

**Currently:** building an open-source pipeline for dense knowledge graph extraction from unstructured text, and thinking hard about how to benchmark it properly — non-deterministic LLM outputs on open-ended tasks need rigorous evaluation frameworks, and that's a gap worth filling.
